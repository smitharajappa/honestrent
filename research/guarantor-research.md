# Guarantors and funding: what is verified (31 Oct - 1 Nov 2026)

Owner: Evidence. Every line says where it came from. "Not verified" means we saw it only on a
blog or search result, not on the company's own site.

## Verified on the company's own site

- **Insurent** (insurent.com/renters): lease guaranty accepted in NY, NJ, MA, IL, VA, MD, NV,
  DC, CA and FL. **Texas is not listed.** Fee, one-time, per lease, one-year guaranty:
  about 70%-90% of one month's rent for US parties; about 98%-110% for non-US parties with
  no US credit history. Students submit ID or passport, visa, school ID, a parent's passport
  and income proof or a bank letter.
- **TheGuarantors** (theguarantors.com): claims "#1 lease guarantee provider nationwide" and
  serves renters with no credit score (customer quote). Publishes no prices and no state list.

## Not verified (blogs and search results only)

- Leap: said to serve international renters in all 50 states. No pricing found.
- Rhino: reported to have merged with Jetty in Feb 2025 and to focus on deposit replacement.
- Fee ranges of 65%-110% of one month's rent (blogs); TheGuarantors "40%-130%" (blog).

## MPOWER Financing (student loans, not a guarantor)

- No cosigner, no collateral loans for international students in the US and Canada.
- Funds go to the school (tuition, university housing, meal plans, health insurance); it cannot
  pay a guarantor or apartment deposit directly.
- `prd.md` says MPOWER paused new loans for 2026. A search found no sign of a pause and 2026
  announcements from MPOWER. **Check mpowerfinancing.com before relying on either.**

## What our five communities publish

Cutters Point, Maya at White Rock (BH Live) and Camden Buckingham, Legacy Park, Legacy Creek:
nothing about guarantors, SSN rules, application fees, admin fees or deposits.
BH Live says applications are screened on income, rental history, credit and criminal history.
So these facts can only come from the leasing offices.

## Consequence for the product

- Guarantor companies accepted and move-in fees are stored as "not confirmed" until a person
  confirms them (see `leasing-office-call-sheet.md`). We never guess them.
- The only number we show for guarantor cost is Insurent's published range, labelled as an
  example from a company that does not serve Texas.
