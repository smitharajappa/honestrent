# Swelt compared with Apartments.com (building page)

Based on the Apartments.com screenshots the team shared (1 Nov 2026). Not a feature audit
of their whole product.

| What a renter wants     | Apartments.com                                                           | Swelt                                                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Jump around a long page | Sticky tab bar (Pricing, Fees and Policies, Location, Transportation...) | Sticky "jump to" bar that highlights the section you are reading                         |
| Price                   | "From" price, fees in a separate "Fees and Policies" tab                 | One top banner: ad price, then required fees, utilities and deals, then the total        |
| Hidden fees             | Listed per property when the manager provides them                       | Itemized, with source and date; "ask the leasing office" when unconfirmed, never guessed |
| Cash to move in         | Not shown as one number                                                  | Move-in cash card, using the renter's own approval answers (kept on the device)          |
| Will they approve me?   | Not shown                                                                | Approval rules and a fit badge for no-SSN and no-US-credit renters (when confirmed)      |
| Map                     | Google map, category tabs, clusters                                      | Open map (OpenStreetMap), category tabs, clusters, fullscreen, directions                |
| Getting around          | Walk, transit and bike scores, nearest airport and rail                  | Typical weekday drive time by hour to UT Dallas, bus stops, walking times                |
| Neighbourhood           | Schools, points of interest, reviews                                     | Nearby places, plus reported-incident counts from official open data (Dallas)            |
| Contact                 | Send message, schedule tour                                              | "I'm interested" lead form                                                               |
| Ranking                 | Sponsored placements                                                     | Never ranked by who pays                                                                 |

## Where Apartments.com is ahead (we do not try to match)

Photos and 3D tours, walk/transit/bike scores, school ratings, reviews, tour scheduling,
and thousands of listings. Our edge is the cost and approval picture for newcomers.
